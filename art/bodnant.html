About this painting

Bodnant captures the relationship between formal gardens and the surrounding Welsh landscape, with the house, terraces and hills combining into a strong sense of place.

This piece is suited to anyone drawn to gardens, historic houses, Welsh landscapes and architectural scenes with atmosphere.
