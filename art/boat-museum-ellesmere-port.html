About this painting

Boat Museum Ellesmere Port reflects the industrial and social history preserved at the canal museum, where working boats, historic buildings and waterways tell the story of life on Britain’s canals.

Ellesmere Port sits at the junction of the Manchester Ship Canal and the Shropshire Union Canal, linking the site to the wider network of waterways across the UK. This painting captures that sense of heritage, place and working history.
