Woodland Stream

A mounted watercolour of a turbulent stream running through a lush green forest in south west France.

Artwork details

A turbulent stream crashing over rugged rocks and through a lush green forest. The scene is at Prise d’Alzeau, south west France, where the stream feeds one of the rills which provide water to the iconic Canal du Midi.

About this painting

Woodland Stream captures the movement and energy of water as it breaks over rocks and cuts through the surrounding woodland.

The location connects to the wider history of the Canal du Midi, with the water feeding one of the rills that help supply this remarkable French canal system.
