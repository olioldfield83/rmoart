Liverpool Waterfront

A framed pen and wash painting of Liverpool’s iconic waterfront.

Artwork details

The iconic Three Graces look out over Liverpool Waterfront, sitting alongside the modern museum building. Together they reflect the rich and diverse history of this historic port.

About this painting

Liverpool Waterfront captures one of the city’s most recognisable views, combining the historic presence of the Three Graces with the contemporary architecture of the waterfront museum.

The piece reflects Liverpool’s maritime heritage, civic architecture and continuing evolution as a cultural waterfront city.
