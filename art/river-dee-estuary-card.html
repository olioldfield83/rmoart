About this card

The River Dee Estuary Shore greeting card features Richard Oldfield's depiction of the estuary's sandbanks and shoreline.

Inspired by the distinctive landscape of the Dee Estuary, the design reflects Richard's interest in coastal scenery, natural landscapes and the changing atmosphere of the Wirral coastline.

This card is particularly suited to anyone who loves the Wirral, the River Dee, coastal landscapes or artwork inspired by familiar local places.
