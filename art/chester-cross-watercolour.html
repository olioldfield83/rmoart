About this painting

Chester Cross was inspired by the historic architecture of the buildings surrounding one of Chester's best-known landmarks.

The painting celebrates the relationship between the city's architectural heritage and the energy of modern city life. Using pen and watercolour, Richard captures the intricate character of the buildings alongside the vibrant atmosphere of this distinctive location.

This piece is particularly suited to anyone who enjoys Chester, historic architecture, cityscapes and original artwork that celebrates places with a strong identity.
