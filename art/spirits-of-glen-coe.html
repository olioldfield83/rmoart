About this painting

Spirits of Glen Coe reflects Richard’s interest in expressive landscapes, texture and atmosphere. Glen Coe was shaped over millennia by the forces of nature and centuries through the tales of defiance and valour the glen invokes the spirits of its rich past.

This piece is suited to anyone drawn to Scotland, mountain landscapes, dramatic skies and original artwork with energy and depth.
