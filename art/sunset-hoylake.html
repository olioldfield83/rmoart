Sunset Hoylake

A mixed media painting inspired by the glow of sunset over Hoylake and the open atmosphere of the Wirral coastline.

Artwork details

Sunset Hoylake captures the dramatic evening light of the Wirral coast, where warm colour, wide skies and the quiet edge of the shore combine to create a strong sense of atmosphere and place.

About this painting

Sunset Hoylake reflects Richard’s interest in coastal landscapes, changing light and the expressive possibilities of mixed media. The composition evokes the calm and drama of evening skies over the Dee Estuary and Wirral shoreline.

This piece is suited to anyone drawn to coastal views, sunsets, the Wirral landscape and original artwork with warmth, movement and atmosphere.
