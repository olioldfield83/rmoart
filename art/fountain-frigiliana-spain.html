About this painting

Fountain, Frigiliana, Spain reflects Richard’s interest in travel, architecture and places with a strong sense of identity. The painting captures the atmosphere of Frigiliana through colour, light and careful observation.

It is a piece for anyone drawn to Spain, village scenes, travel-inspired artwork and original watercolour paintings with a sense of place.
