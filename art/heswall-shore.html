Original oil painting

Heswall Shore

An oil painting inspired by the boatyard at Heswall Shore, looking out towards the Welsh hills.

Artwork details

The boatyard at Heswall Shore provides a safe refuge for boats and a final resting place for many abandoned boats. Looking out to the Welsh hills, it is an inspiring and dramatic place.

Status: Available
Price: Price on enquiry

  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Size: 50cm × 40cm
  • Subject: Heswall Shore, Wirral
  • Artist: Richard Oldfield

About this painting

Heswall Shore captures the atmosphere of a working shoreline where boats, weathered materials and open views combine to create a strong sense of place.

This piece is suited to anyone drawn to the Wirral coastline, maritime subjects, boatyards and original landscape paintings with local character.
