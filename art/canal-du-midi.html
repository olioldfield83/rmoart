Original oil painting

Canal du Midi

An oil painting inspired by the tree-lined Canal du Midi and the cool corridor it creates for passing boats.

Artwork details

The Canal winds its way from Bordeaux in the west to Narbonne on the Mediterranean coast. It is lined with ancient Poplar and Willow trees, forming a cool corridor for the passing boats.

Status: Available
Price: Price on enquiry

  • Medium: Oil on canvas
  • Size: 50cm × 40cm
  • Subject: Canal du Midi, France
  • Artist: Richard Oldfield

About this painting

Canal du Midi captures the calm rhythm of the canal, with the trees, water and passing boats combining to create a strong sense of shade, movement and place.

This piece is suited to anyone drawn to French waterways, canal scenes, southern France and original landscape paintings with a peaceful travelling atmosphere.
