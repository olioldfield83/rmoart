Albert Dock

A bright pen and wash piece capturing the iconic Albert Dock at the heart of Liverpool’s history.

Artwork details

The iconic Albert Dock has a rich history at the heart of Liverpool’s past. This bright pen and wash piece reflects its vibrant past and its repurposing into a flamboyant centre for living and entertainment.

About this sketch

Albert Dock reflects Richard’s interest in Liverpool’s architecture, waterfront history and urban spaces that have evolved over time.

This piece is suited to anyone who enjoys Liverpool, maritime heritage, historic docklands and pen and wash artwork with strong local character.
